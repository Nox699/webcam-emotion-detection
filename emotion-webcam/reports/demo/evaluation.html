Interpretation

Happy, Sad, and Neutral are facial-expression labels. A face alone does not provide a reliable measurement of a person's internal emotional state. Eye openness and smile intensity are rough Haar-detector proxies. The personal model also uses 900 HOG image features.

Lighting, face pose, glasses, camera quality, small training sets, and ambiguous expressions can change predictions. A high softmax score is a model score, not a calibrated probability of someone's emotion. The application suppresses low-score and poor-quality predictions.

Future improvements

Use a larger consented dataset with person-disjoint test sets, facial landmarks, stronger alignment, probability calibration, and a validated CNN. Evaluate additional expressions separately before adding new labels.
