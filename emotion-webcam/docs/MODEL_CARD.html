Model card

Intended use: course demonstration of supervised learning and local classification of consented, posed facial expressions for one laptop user.

Classes: Happy, Neutral, Sad. Alphabetical order is used consistently in training, JSON inference, bars, reports, and confusion matrices.

Architecture: StandardScaler followed by multiclass logistic regression (lbfgs, max_iter=2000, balanced class weights; C=1 for simulation and C=0.2 for personal HOG models). The scaler is fit on training samples only. The held-out evaluation model is deployed without a full-dataset refit.

Demo data: 800 samples per class, generated from overlapping Gaussian distributions for two normalized feature proxies, clipped to [0, 1]. Seed 42. 80/20 stratified split. Demo distributions are an illustrative assumption, not empirically estimated facial-expression measurements.

Personal data: explicitly labeled features from complete recording sessions. All three classes must have at least 10 samples in each session. At least five sessions are required. GroupShuffleSplit holds out 20% of session IDs (rounded upward); this is 20% of sessions, not necessarily 20% of rows when session sizes differ. No face images are stored by collection.

Feature extraction: OpenCV frontal-face Haar cascade; eye and smile Haar cascades on a 96×96 equalized face; rough eye rectangle ratio and smile width proxies. Personal model adds HOG on a 48×48 CLAHE-preprocessed face (16×16 blocks, 8×8 stride/cells, nine bins, 900 coefficients). HOG captures image gradients; it is not a facial landmark estimator. Normal operation uses one frontal face at a time.

Live postprocessing: elapsed-time exponential smoothing (0.35-second time constant), reset on face loss, poor quality, IoU below 0.3, or a gap above one second. Highest score below 0.6 displays Uncertain by default. Brightness <35 or >225, contrast <12, or normalized Laplacian variance <25 suppresses prediction. Thresholds are practical defaults, not calibrated clinical criteria. IoU continuity does not verify person identity: switching people at the same location can retain smoothing briefly. Press R to clear it.

Evaluation: accuracy, per-class precision/recall/F1, macro/weighted averages, confusion matrix, exact split indices and session IDs. Metrics evaluate raw held-out classifier predictions, without live smoothing or quality rejection. They do not measure temporal response or rejection-adjusted live accuracy. The report states its data source explicitly.

Known risks to validity: recording frames are correlated; session holdout reduces leakage but does not solve small-sample uncertainty. Training on one person is not evidence for performance on other people. Posed expressions are not spontaneous emotion. Sad/Neutral ambiguity, face alignment, pose, glasses, blur, lighting, and detector errors can dominate. The model can learn nuisance cues rather than expression. High scores can be wrong and are not calibrated emotion probabilities. No demographic, clinical, hiring, surveillance, or safety-critical validation was performed.

Privacy: numerical HOG features remain face-derived information. Keep the project local and protect personal.csv. Collection saves no raw images. Optional CLI --output is an explicit annotated-video export.

Release gate: complete LOCAL_ACCEPTANCE.md on the intended Windows laptop. For deployment beyond this assignment, obtain a larger consented real dataset, person-disjoint evaluations, appropriate intended-use review, and independent real-world validation.
